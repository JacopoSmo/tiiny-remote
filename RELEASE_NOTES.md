Download the .apk below on your phone and open it to install or update (or in the app: Settings → App updates → Update app).

## What's new
- **Status panel:** the NPU memory bar is split per loaded model, each in its own colour (also shown as a dot before the model's name).
- **Chat:** Share ▾ under every answer: share or copy the question and the answer together, or the answer alone.
- **Chat:** when an answer breaks off because the Tiiny's NPU just restarted after a fault, the chat says so ("Send your question again").
- **Speed test:** with 3 or 4 runs, a clear outlier is left out of the average, and the result says which.
- **Log:** long-press entries to choose just some, then Share or Copy only those (an error with what came before and after it).
- **Log:** Tiiny stats adds the Tiiny's own request statistics for its system service.
- **Log:** an NPU fault the Tiiny still keeps in its status from earlier is marked as an earlier one, not as a new one.
- **About:** your Tiiny's TiinyOS, service and gateway versions and hardware; "Copy for a bug report" includes the TiinyOS version.
