Download the .apk below on your phone and open it to install or update (or in the app: Settings → App updates → Update app).

## What's new
- **VPN:** when WireGuard is asleep, the app now opens it and asks up to three times (about 25 seconds) before giving up, instead of once. A just-woken WireGuard sometimes ignored the first request.
- Tip: to avoid the waiting altogether, set WireGuard's battery use to "Unrestricted" in Android's settings.
