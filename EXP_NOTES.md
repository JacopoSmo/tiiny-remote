**Experimental** — installs as a separate app, "Tiiny Remote Exp" (orange "exp" ribbon on its icon), beside the normal Tiiny Remote, with its own settings. Newest features first; may still have rough edges. Download the .apk below on your phone and open it to install or update (or in the app: Settings → App updates → Update app).

## 4.0 (what's new compared with the normal app 3.2)
- **Automate:** processes (at a set time or on tap: news, web search, calendar or email → a model → a notification) and agents (a goal and tools; the model chooses its steps). Calendar and email (IMAP, read-only) are connected separately. Runs on the phone, or around the clock on your NAS with the NAS agent (`nas-agent/` in this repository, a small Docker service).
- **Create, redesigned:** 3D · Audio · Image · Text · Video on top, each with its choices (Image: Create, Describe, Edit, OCR; Audio: Voice, Loop, Song, ASR; Text: Classification, Embedding, Reranking). Opens on Image → Create; Back returns to where you were.
- **Gallery:** everything made, by kind and choice, searchable, newest or oldest first, grouped by day, month or year, pictures as a grid; links to and from Create.
- **Translate:** typed text (language detected), a photo (menu, sign; camera or gallery) or speech; chat models are marked "Translates" in Models.
- **Speed test:** 1 or 3–5 runs with a quarter-second pause, up to 2 outliers of 5 left out, and a History of all tests (best pairs, model speeds, table).
- **Chat:** History (clock, top right) with each chat's last model; "Use it" or "Load it" when you reopen one. ＋ beside the message attaches an image (from the phone or a new photo, for models that see images) or a file (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text; read on the phone). 🎤 dictates a message with the Tiiny's speech-to-text model. The Save sign (top right) is green while chats are saved and red in incognito.
- **Stay connected in the background** (Settings): a silent notification, the Tiiny checked every minute and unlocked after a restart even with the app closed.
- **Code (new):** a coding model on your Tiiny works with you on projects kept on the phone. 💬 Ask (questions, snippets; it may read the files), 🛠 Build (it writes and changes the files itself), 🎓 Learn (step by step). Files to read or edit yourself, Preview for web pages, Share as .zip, GitHub (copy a repository in, push a project in one commit, make a new private repository). Models → Types → Coding shows the models good at code.
- **Sidebar:** Chat, Code, Create, Gallery, Automate, Translate; then Activity, Models, Settings. ⚙ at its top: which screen the app opens on (or where you left off) and the order of the top part. ⏻ at its bottom shuts the app down completely (asks whether to unload loaded models first).
- **Getting more out of the NPU:** a model is never loaded while another one is writing (it waits, at most 5 minutes; the NPU faults were timeouts on the link those loads use). The helper's jobs (chat names, follow-up ideas) wait until the answer is finished. Long attached files are condensed after the first answer, so later questions don't send them whole again. Settings → Always ready keeps speech-to-text and/or OCR loaded whenever there's room. Speed statistics (Settings → Fast helper model → 📊 Statistics) fill themselves from normal chat answers (alone, and beside another model).
- **Help:** a short welcome tour at the first start (Settings → About → Welcome tour shows it again), and a ? beside every page's title that walks you through that page.
- **Chat:** what you type is kept per chat when you leave the app or the screen; a tip on fast A3B versus careful 27B models in a new chat; a tighter ＋ menu.
- **VPN:** another app's VPN (DuckDuckGo's App Tracking Protection, say) is no longer taken for WireGuard: the app learns WireGuard's own address the first time it switches it on, or when WireGuard reaches the Tiiny away from home.
- **NPU watch:** the log notes each time the models on the NPU change (also when Tiiny Studio loads one), and an NPU fault records what was loaded at that moment. A new fault makes the status strip pulse red until you tap it. The strip's RAM is the CPU's own 32 GB; the models use the NPU's 48 GB (together the Tiiny's 80 GB).
- **Smaller:** simpler menus; long dropdown lists (languages, models…) no longer run off the screen; drawn pictograms for the new pages and one meaning per pictogram (legend in Settings → Labels); a slightly larger title in the compact top bar; Activity's log titled "Logs".
