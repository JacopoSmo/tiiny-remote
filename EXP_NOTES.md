**Experimental** — installs as a separate app, "Tiiny Remote Exp" (orange "exp" ribbon on its icon), beside the normal Tiiny Remote, with its own settings. Newest features first; may still have rough edges. Download the .apk below on your phone and open it to install or update (or in the app: Settings → App updates → Update app).

## 4.0 (what's new compared with the normal app 3.2)
- **Automations:** processes (at a set time or on tap: news, web search, calendar or email → a model → a notification) and agents (a goal and tools; the model chooses its steps). Calendar and email (IMAP, read-only) are connected separately. Runs on the phone, or around the clock on your NAS with the NAS agent (`nas-agent/` in this repository, a small Docker service).
- **Create, redesigned:** 3D · Audio · Image · Text · Video on top, each with its choices (Image: Create, Describe, Edit, OCR; Audio: Voice, Loop, Song, ASR; Text: Classification, Embedding, Reranking). Opens on Image → Create; Back returns to where you were.
- **Creations:** everything made, by kind and choice, searchable, newest or oldest first, grouped by day, month or year, pictures as a grid; links to and from Create.
- **Translate:** typed text (language detected), a photo (menu, sign; camera or gallery) or speech; chat models are marked "Translates" in Models.
- **Speed test:** 1 or 3–5 runs with short pauses, up to 2 outliers of 5 left out, and a History of all tests (best pairs, model speeds, table).
- **Chat:** History (clock, top right) with each chat's last model; "Use it" or "Load it" when you reopen one.
- **Stay connected in the background** (Settings): a silent notification, the Tiiny checked every minute and unlocked after a restart even with the app closed.
- **Smaller:** simpler menus; long dropdown lists (languages, models…) no longer run off the screen; drawn pictograms for the new pages and one meaning per pictogram (legend in Settings → Labels); a slightly larger title in the compact top bar; Activity's log titled "Logs".
